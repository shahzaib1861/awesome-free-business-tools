# Awesome Free Business Tools

A curated list of free and open-source tools for small businesses: invoicing, CRM, analytics, payroll records, shipping and more.

> Disclosure: this list is maintained by the team behind Saasnm, whose free tools are included below alongside other options.

## Invoicing and accounting

- [Akaunting](https://akaunting.com/) - Open-source, self-hostable accounting software.
- [Invoice Ninja](https://invoiceninja.com/) - Open-source invoicing and billing platform.
- [Saasnm Invoice Generator](https://saasnm.com/invoice-creation/) - Free online invoice generator with customizable details.
- [Wave](https://www.waveapps.com/) - Free core invoicing and accounting for small businesses (some features are paid).

## CRM and business apps

- [Odoo Community](https://www.odoo.com/) - Open-source Community edition of a modular business suite.
- [Saasnm SuiteCRM Guide](https://saasnm.com/blog/suitecrm-guide/) - An honest guide to what SuiteCRM does and what self-hosting takes.
- [SuiteCRM](https://suitecrm.com/) - Open-source CRM.

## Analytics and reporting

- [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/) - Free web analytics.
- [Matomo](https://matomo.org/) - Open-source web analytics you can self-host.
- [Metabase](https://www.metabase.com/) - Open-source business intelligence and dashboards.
- [Saasnm Business Analytics](https://saasnm.com/business-analytics/) - Free online tool to track performance and sales.

## Payroll records

- [Saasnm Pay Stub Generator](https://saasnm.com/pay-stub/) - Free pay stub generator. Always verify figures against your local tax rules.

## Shipping and fulfillment

- [Saasnm Shipping Label Maker](https://saasnm.com/shipping-label/) - Free online shipping label maker.

## Branding and naming

- [Saasnm Business Name Generator](https://saasnm.com/business-name/) - Free generator for creative, brand-ready business names.

## AI and productivity

- [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/) - Free and open-source office suite.
- [Saasnm AI Prompt Generator](https://saasnm.com/ai-prompt/) - Free tool for writing structured AI prompts.

## Construction and estimating

- [Saasnm Concrete Calculator](https://saasnm.com/concrete-calculator/) - Free calculator for cubic yards, bag counts and cost estimates.

## Contributing

Pull requests are welcome. An entry should be free or have a meaningful free tier, include a one-line description, and be listed alphabetically within its section. Open an issue to suggest a new category.
